# Curriculum & Stage Progression

To prevent cognitive overload, the learning pathway is split into **46 bite-sized stages**, each introducing **3 to 6 characters**.

---

## 1. Design Rationale

Early iterations of traditional curricula often group all 20 Dakuten characters or all 46 Katakana characters into single stages. This produces an abrupt difficulty cliff that stalls learning.

Our 46-stage pathway enforces a steady cognitive load:
- **Hiragana**: 20 stages (Gojūon, Dakuten, Handakuten, Yōon).
- **Katakana**: 26 stages (Gojūon, Dakuten, Handakuten, Yōon, and Extended Katakana).
- **Cumulative Practice**: In Guided Mode, each new stage adds to all previously learned characters, ensuring earlier knowledge remains reinforced.

## 4. Mastery Requirements

A stage unlocks the next stage when all characters in the current stage satisfy:
- **Reviews**: At least 3 successful repetitions.
- **Streak**: 3 consecutive correct answers without mistakes.
- **Speed**: Average response latency below 4.0 seconds.
